# Cancellation Policy

> DEMO DOCUMENT: a fictional policy used for development. Replace it with the real company policy.

## Cancelling before shipping
An order can be cancelled for free as long as it has not shipped yet. Cancellations are reviewed and confirmed by a member of our support team.

## Cancelling after shipping
Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled. When it arrives you can return it for a refund under the refund policy, within the 30-day return window.

## How to cancel
Contact support with your order number and the email address used for the order. Our team will check the order status and confirm whether it can still be cancelled.
