# Shipping Policy

> DEMO DOCUMENT: a fictional policy used for development. Replace it with the real company policy.

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Delivery times start when the order ships, not when it is placed.

## Tracking your order
When your order ships you receive a tracking number by email. You can follow the parcel on the carrier's website with that number.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address only before the order has shipped. Once the order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

## Lost or delayed parcels
If your tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact support. A parcel that has not arrived 14 days after shipping is treated as not received.
