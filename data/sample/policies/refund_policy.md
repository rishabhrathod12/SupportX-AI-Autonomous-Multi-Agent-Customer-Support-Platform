# Refund Policy

> DEMO DOCUMENT: a fictional policy used for development. Replace it with the real company policy.

## Return window
Items can be returned for a refund within 30 days after delivery. The 30 days start on the day the carrier marks the order as delivered. After 30 days we cannot accept a return.

## Damaged or not as described
If an item arrives damaged, or is not as described on the product page, you can request a refund within the 30-day return window. Please include a photo of the damage so our team can review the request.

## Changed your mind
You can also return an item because you changed your mind, within the same 30-day window, as long as the item is not final sale.

## Final sale items
Items marked final sale cannot be returned or refunded. The product page shows this label before you buy.

## Orders that have not arrived
If an order was shipped but has not arrived 14 days after shipping, you can report it as not received and ask for a refund. If the carrier shows the order as delivered, a member of our team will investigate first.

## How refunds are approved
Every refund request is reviewed and approved by a member of our support team. The system can only recommend a refund; a person makes the decision. Only one refund request can be open for an order at a time. After approval, the money goes back to the original payment method.
